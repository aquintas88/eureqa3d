## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Use keywords naturally** — Don't keyword stuff
- **Vary anchor text** — Mix short and long keywords
- **Internal links** — Link using keywords (e.g., "modelos 3D para traumatología" not "click here")
- **Update existing content** — Don't just add new pages; optimize what exists
- **Monitor rankings** — Google Search Console tells you what's ranking + CTR
